Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as I can get to the back of the house, you do not need to be in. I text the night before so you can leave the side gate unlocked.

Why does the first clean cost more?

Glass that has not been cleaned for a while takes a lot longer to bring back than glass on a six week rhythm. The first clean is one and a half to two times the normal price, and I will tell you exactly what it is before I start.

Am I tied into anything?

No contract, no minimum, no cancellation fee. Tell me to stop and I stop.

What if it rains?

I still clean. Rain is clean water and it does not undo the job. If the weather is bad enough to be unsafe I will move you to the next day and text you.

What if you cannot get round the back?

I will clean the front, charge you less for that visit, and catch the back next time.

How do I pay?

Card or bank transfer after each clean. Details come with your confirmation text.
